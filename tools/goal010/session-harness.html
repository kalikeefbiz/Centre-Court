FIND YOUR RHYTHM

Make the next shot.

Move into range. Use WASD / arrows, or the left joystick. Track the ball’s shadow and the landing ring.

One HIT control. Tap for slice, hold and release for power, or double tap for a lob. Space and the left mouse button use the same gestures. Start your swing before the ball reaches your racket. Drag while pressing to aim; the power bar shows your charge. Your thumb can travel beyond HIT while aiming; only release commits the shot.

Place your return. Drag from HIT to aim left / right and up / down for depth. J / L aim on desktop. I / K change depth on desktop. Mouse position across the court sets aim. Desktop keys 1 Drive, 2 Slice, or 3 Lob override the next shot. Forehand, backhand, volleys and overheads follow the incoming ball.

Signature. Build charge with returns and points. When full, tap SIGNATURE to arm the next successful rally contact. A miss costs no charge. Heat builds at 8 returns and ignites at 12; it changes feedback only.

Serve diagonally. Hold and release to serve. Two faults lose the point. A net cord landing in the correct box is a let. Lines are in.

Quick set: first to 2 games, win by 2, tiebreak at 2–2. Standard: best of 3 sets, tiebreak at 6–6. Practice runs endless rallies. Escape pauses. C changes camera.
