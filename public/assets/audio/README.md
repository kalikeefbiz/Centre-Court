# Court audio licenses

All recorded samples are edited from CC0 1.0 sounds. Sources, exact HQ-preview
URLs and cut times are in `sources.json`. CC0 permits commercial reuse and edits.
The original WAV downloads require a Freesound account; these edits use the public
HQ MP3 previews, not falsely claimed lossless masters.

- jamesdrake89: Tennis Forehand 2, Tennis Serves 1, Tennis Slices 2.
- drivr3joe: TennisBallBounce.wav (ball bouncing on ground; surface unspecified).
- baidonovan: Rubber Shoe Squeak.wav (floor surface unspecified).
- SpliceSound: Footsteps, Tile, Male Tennis Shoes, Fast Pace.wav.

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The footstep recording is tennis footwear on tile, not a claimed court recording.
The bounce recording's surface is unspecified. Both provide licensed physical
transients; verified hard-court field recordings would improve authenticity.
Net contact uses a quiet recorded racket transient plus a synthesized low accent.
Point, heat and signature cues are intentionally synthesized interface sounds.
The existing crowd bed is synthesized. No character vocals ship: each athlete has
an independent empty voice manifest and context/probability/cooldown hooks.
