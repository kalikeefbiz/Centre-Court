Flight uses quadratic drag F=0.5*rho*Cd*A*v^2 and spin-ratio-dependent lift along
omega cross velocity, capped at Cl=0.28. Bounce uses Coulomb friction acting on
contact-point slip with hollow-sphere inertia I=(2/3)*m*r^2: the no-slip impulse
per unit mass is -2/5 times slip, capped at mu*(1+e)*normal impact speed. Normal
restitution decreases with impact speed. Coefficients are centrally tunable game
calibration, not claims of an empirically fitted professional-tennis simulator.

Kira's source GLB, all original animation tracks, skin, mesh, textures, rig and

derived only from the game's existing owned assets using the included sampling
tool and native AnimTrack data. No external animation or commercial game data
were downloaded or copied.
